## Vietnam Legal Counsel Review Checklist

This reference checklist provides detailed statutory compliance, contract vetting, PDPL 2025 personal data protection, AI Law No. 134/2025/QH15 risk governance, and regulatory review criteria for the Vietnam Legal Counsel role to meet 2027 Agentic SWE and Vietnamese statutory legal standards.

### 1. Jurisdiction & Entity Verification
- **Entity Corporate Form & Legal Representation Authority**: Confirm candidate corporate form (Joint Stock Company, Multi-Member LLC, Single-Member LLC, Branch, Representative Office), ERC/IRC registration status, business scope against the negative investment list, and authorized Legal Representative (*Người đại diện theo pháp luật*) authority allocations per corporate charter under Article 12.2 Law on Enterprises 2020 to prevent unauthorized corporate commitments by co-representatives.
- **90-Day Capital Contribution & Related-Party Approval Thresholds**: Verify compliance with the statutory 90-day charter capital contribution window from ERC issuance (Articles 47.2, 75.2, 113.1 Law on Enterprises 2020) and mandatory 30-day capital reduction filings upon subscriber default; audit Related-Party Transactions (RPT) against Article 167 approval thresholds (Board of Directors < 35% vs General Meeting of Shareholders >= 35%) with mandatory interested party recusal.

### 2. Statutory Basis & Gazette Verification
- **Official Gazette (*Công báo*) Citation & Source Versioning**: Verify all governing statutory provisions (Civil Code 2015, Commercial Law 2005, Law on Enterprises 2020 as amended 2022, Labor Code 2019, Law on Intellectual Property as amended 2022, Law on Electronic Transactions 2023, Law on Cybersecurity 2018, Personal Data Protection Law No. 91/2025/QH15, Decree 356/2025/ND-CP, Law on Artificial Intelligence No. 134/2025/QH15) against official gazette promulgation numbers, effective dates, and active amendments. Strictly disallow reliance on unverified secondary summaries, blog commentary, or repealed legal instruments.
- **Supreme People's Court Precedents (*Án lệ*) & Judicial Interpretations**: Cross-reference contentious contract clauses, non-compete agreements (NCA), penalty enforcements, or arbitral matters against published Supreme People's Court Precedents (e.g., Precedent No. 69/2023/AL on commercial dispute jurisdiction, Precedent No. 42/2021/AL on consumer rights) and relevant Judicial Council Resolutions to ensure legal positions withstand judicial and arbitral scrutiny.

### 3. Commercial Contract Compliance
- **Statutory Penalty Cap & Tripartite Remedy Architecture**: Enforce the mandatory 8% statutory penalty ceiling on the breached obligation portion under Article 301 Commercial Law 2005 for commercial contracts (*lex specialis*); reject void uncapped penalty clauses; structure enforceable tripartite remedies distinguishing statutory penalties (max 8%), actual proven damages (Article 302), and civil deposit forfeitures (Article 328 Civil Code 2015).
- **Dispute Resolution Escalation & Arbitral Forum Selection**: Verify multi-tiered dispute escalation clauses (Negotiation -> Formal Mediation -> Binding Arbitration or Court); ensure valid arbitration agreements under Law on Commercial Arbitration 2010 (Article 19 severability, clearly designated arbitral institution such as VIAC or SIAC, seat of arbitration, language, and governing substantive law); monitor the 9-month commercial statute of limitations (Article 319 Commercial Law 2005) and structure timely formal notices of breach.

### 4. PDPL 2025 Compliance
- **Verifiable Consent Logging & Non-Bundling Verification**: Audit consent collection mechanisms across digital and non-digital data processing against PDPL No. 91/2025/QH15 and Decree No. 356/2025/ND-CP; verify that timestamp, substantive content/purpose, and unambiguous affirmative act of consent are immutably logged; strictly disallow pre-ticked checkboxes, default consent, or bundling consent with general terms of service.
- **Conditional Business Eligibility & MPS Certification**: Verify whether the entity provides commercial personal data processing services as a business line; confirm acquisition of the mandatory Certificate of Business Eligibility issued by the Ministry of Public Security (MPS) prior to commencing commercial data processing services; verify designation of Data Protection Officer (DPO) and organizational data protection operational unit.
- **Cross-Border Transfer & Revenue-Based Penalty Mitigation**: Review cross-border data transfer impact assessments and dossiers prior to transmitting Vietnamese citizens' personal data abroad; verify compliance with transfer prerequisites to eliminate exposure to statutory revenue-based fines of up to 5% of prior fiscal year's total revenue; enforce operational procedures to acknowledge and initiate action on data subject requests (access, correction, deletion, withdrawal) within the statutory 2-working-day SLA. Note backward compatibility: contracts executed under Decree 13 remain governed by that regime until renegotiated.

### 5. AI Law 134/2025 Compliance
- **3-Tier AI Risk Classification & Pre-Market Assessment**: Classify all AI systems, agent workflows, autonomous agents, and automated decision engines under the statutory 3-tier framework of Law No. 134/2025/QH15 (Low-risk, Medium-risk, High-risk); verify that risk classification occurs prior to production deployment or issuing any compliance opinion; strictly prohibit unclassified AI deployments.
- **High-Risk Conformity Assessment, Portal Registration & Local Representative**: For High-risk AI systems, verify execution of mandatory third-party or internal conformity assessments; ensure registration with the National AI Database portal; establish continuous post-market monitoring and audit logging; verify local presence or designated authorized representative in Vietnam for foreign AI providers; track statutory grace periods (18 months until September 2027 for Healthcare, Education, and Banking/Finance; 12 months until March 2027 for all other sectors).
- **Incident Reporting Timelines & Transparency Labeling**: Enforce statutory incident notification procedures: mandatory formal reporting to the Ministry of Science and Technology (MoST) within 72 hours for urgent/uncontrolled safety events and within 5 working days for serious incidents; verify user transparency disclosures informing users when they interact with AI systems, and enforce standardized cryptographic watermarking or metadata labeling for AI-generated content (text, audio, image, video); advise on administrative fines up to VND 2 billion and revenue-based penalties.

### 6. Governance & HITL Gates
- **Non-Discretionary Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate Execution**: Strictly forbid autonomous agent execution or transmission of binding corporate agreements, external formal legal opinions to third parties, or regulatory submissions to state authorities (MoST, A05 MPS, Business Registration Office) without explicit cryptographic or recorded human sign-off from the Legal Representative or Chief Legal Officer.
- **Segregation of Duties & Restricted Data Masking**: Enforce absolute segregation between internal advisory drafting and authorized corporate commitment; ensure citizen identification numbers (CCCD/CMND), financial accounts, biometric templates, and unmasked corporate trade secrets are fully redacted or pseudonymized before artifact emission; verify emission of schema-valid `legal-compliance-review.json` or `legal-opinion-contract.json` artifacts.
