## SRE Review Checklist

This reference checklist provides detailed reliability engineering, error budget governance, telemetry observability, chaos verification, and incident management review criteria for the Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) role to meet 2026–2027 SOTA reliability engineering standards. It synthesizes Google SRE Workbook MWMBR alerting, kernel-level eBPF socket profiling, OpenTelemetry v1.30+ GenAI semantic conventions, proactive automated chaos verification, and machine-readable incident postmortems.

### 1. Service Level Objectives & MWMBR Error Budget Governance
- **Multi-Window Multi-Burn-Rate (MWMBR) Alerting Architecture**: Verify that error budget alerting implements Google SRE Workbook MWMBR alerting across short and long time windows to eliminate alert fatigue while rapidly detecting critical budget consumption. Ensure alerts evaluate dual windows: 1h/5m for 14.4x burn rate (2% budget consumption in 1h, P1 page), 6h/30m for 6x burn rate (5% budget consumption in 6h, P2 page), and 3d/2h for 1x burn rate (10% budget consumption in 3 days, ticket). Verify that PromQL queries implement empty vector coalescing (`or on() vector(0)`) to prevent false alerts during low-traffic windows.
- **Error Budget Policy & Automated Deployment Freeze Gates**: Verify that every production service has a documented error budget policy linking error budget consumption to deployment velocity. Confirm that automated gates in CI/CD and GitOps halt non-reliability feature deployments upon 100% budget consumption, triggering a mandatory Reliability Sprint. Verify that release freeze bypasses require recorded dual sign-off from Product Manager and Engineering Director.
- **User-Centric SLI/SLO Formulation & AI-Specific Metrics**: Confirm SLIs reflect Critical User Journeys (CUJs) rather than internal component uptime. For AI/ML and GenAI workloads, confirm mandatory coverage of AI-specific SLOs: Time To First Token (TTFT P95 < 1.5s), output quality/accuracy score (>= 90% over rolling 24h), token cost budget per request, context window saturation (< 85%), and automated model degradation/drift alerts triggering rollback.

### 2. OpenTelemetry & eBPF Telemetry Observability
- **OpenTelemetry v1.30+ Distributed Tracing & W3C Context**: Verify unbroken propagation of W3C `traceparent` and `tracestate` headers across all HTTP, gRPC, and asynchronous event boundaries (Kafka, Dapr, RabbitMQ). Ensure span hierarchies capture database, cache, and external HTTP hops with standardized attributes; verify zero orphan spans across asynchronous worker queues.
- **OpenTelemetry GenAI Semantic Conventions**: Verify that AI and LLM inference endpoints are instrumented using stable OpenTelemetry v1.30+ GenAI semantic conventions (`gen_ai.system`, `gen_ai.request.model`, `gen_ai.response.model`, `gen_ai.usage.input_tokens`, `gen_ai.usage.output_tokens`, `gen_ai.usage.tokens`, and TTFT). Confirm that prompts and completions containing sensitive data or PII are masked before span export.
- **Kernel-Level eBPF Telemetry (Cilium Hubble & Beyla)**: Confirm kernel-level eBPF socket and network flow monitoring without sidecar proxy overhead. Verify that TCP retransmit rates, connection resets, DNS resolution latencies, and socket buffer queue saturation are continuously measured and correlated with application connection pool metrics.

### 3. Incident Detection, Containment & Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys)
- **High-Fidelity Detection & Four Golden Signals Coverage**: Verify that monitoring dashboards and alert rules encompass the Four Golden Signals (Latency, Traffic, Errors, Saturation) for all tier-1 services. Confirm that alert notifications provide actionable links: direct links to dashboards, correlated trace queries, and executable runbooks; verify elimination of noisy or non-actionable pages.
- **Rapid Mitigation & Blast Radius Containment**: Confirm that immediate mitigation actions (traffic shifting, feature flag disabling, autoscaling, canary drain, circuit breaker trip) are strictly decoupled from root-cause debugging. Measure Mean Time to Mitigation (MTTM); verify that mitigations do not trigger cascading downstream failures across service dependencies.
- **5 Whys Root Cause Analysis & Standardized Failure Taxonomy**: Verify that postmortem investigations execute recursive 5 Whys causal analysis, rejecting superficial conclusions ("human error", "network glitch"). Confirm failure categorization into standardized taxonomies (infrastructure_outage, software_defect, dependency_failure, configuration_drift, capacity_exhaustion, ai_model_degradation).

### 4. Chaos Engineering & Steady-State Failure Injection
- **Steady-State Hypothesis Formulation & Verification**: Confirm that chaos experiments define quantifiable steady-state metrics (P99 latency, request throughput, error rate) measured before, during, and after failure injection. Verify that distributed systems self-heal and return to steady state within declared RTO thresholds without human manual intervention.
- **Automated Fault Injection Matrix (Chaos Mesh / Litmus)**: Verify execution of automated fault injection covering pod evictions, network latency/jitter, network partitioning (split-brain), CPU/memory saturation, disk I/O pressure, and DNS resolution failures. Confirm coverage of upstream dependency and cloud provider outage simulations.
- **Blast Radius Guardrails & Emergency Abort Triggers**: Verify that failure injection experiments are strictly constrained to canary pools, non-critical shards, or controlled environments. Confirm that automated abort triggers immediately terminate chaos experiments and restore baseline state if production error budget burn exceeds 1x or synthetic canaries fail.

### 5. Automated Runbooks, Toil Reduction & Capacity Planning
- **Diagnostic Action Trees & Safe Automated Execution**: Confirm that runbooks are structured as deterministic diagnostic decision trees. Verify that automated runbooks feature dry-run simulation mode, confirmation checkpoints, and immediate human on-call interruptibility (kill switch); verify that irreversible destructive actions are permanently blocked from autonomous execution.
- **Operational Toil Budgeting & Runbook Auditing**: Verify that operational toil (repetitive, manual, automatable work) is tracked and capped at < 50% of SRE capacity. Confirm that every production alert maps to a validated, searchable runbook; verify post-incident runbook audits after every SEV-1/SEV-2 event.
- **Predictive Capacity Planning & Saturation Modeling**: Confirm proactive resource capacity modeling (CPU, memory, disk IOPS, database connections, GPU VRAM and KV cache). Verify saturation alerting configured at 70–80% thresholds before non-linear performance cliffs occur.

### 6. Machine Contract Artifacts & Postmortem Action Tracking
- **JSON Schema Draft 2020-12 Contract Conformity**: Verify that incident reports strictly validate against `contracts/schemas/incident-report.json`. Confirm presence of required fields: discriminator (`contract_type: incident-report`), quantitative metrics (MTTD, MTTM, MTTR in seconds), and error budget impact fields (`burn_rate_peak`, `budget_consumed_percentage`, `slo_breached`).
- **Distributed Trace Context Correlation**: Confirm that postmortem artifacts embed W3C distributed trace identifiers (`root_trace_id`, `culprit_span_id`), enabling 1-click correlation from the postmortem record to exact OpenTelemetry distributed traces.
- **Closed-Loop Preventative Action Item Governance**: Verify that all postmortem action items include unique IDs, designated owner roles (e.g., DevOps Engineer, Backend Developer, Data Engineer), priority ratings, target completion dates, and verifiable success criteria. Confirm that incidents cannot be marked closed while P1 action items remain untracked.
