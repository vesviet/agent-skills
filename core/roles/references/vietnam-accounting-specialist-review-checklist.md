## Vietnam Accounting Specialist Review Checklist

This reference checklist provides detailed accounting, tax compliance, e-invoice verification, 3-way matching, transfer pricing, and period-end closing criteria for the Vietnam Accounting Specialist role to meet 2027 Agentic SWE and Vietnamese statutory accounting standards.

### 1. VAS vs VFRS Dual-Reporting Governance & Chart of Accounts
- **Statutory Regime Determination**: Confirm candidate accounting regime (Circular 200/2014/TT-BTC for large/medium enterprises, Circular 133/2016/TT-BTC for SMEs, Circular 132/2018/TT-BTC for micro-enterprises) based on charter capital, listing status, employee headcount, and revenue.
- **Decision 345/QD-BTC VFRS Roadmap Compliance**: Maintain a dual-layer reporting architecture for enterprises subject to IFRS/VFRS transition; preserve statutory VAS general ledgers while executing deterministic reconciliation adjustments for IFRS 16 (Lease capitalization), IFRS 9 (Expected Credit Loss), and IFRS 15 (Performance-obligation revenue recognition).
- **Chart of Accounts Integrity**: Enforce strict account structure rules (4-digit accounts under Circular 200; 3-digit simplified accounts under Circular 133). Block invalid accounts under Circular 133 (e.g., TK 621, 622, 623, 627, 641 are forbidden; production costs pool in TK 154 and selling expenses in TK 6421).
- **Statutory Books Completeness**: Verify that General Journal (Sổ Nhật ký chung), General Ledger (Sổ Cái), and Detailed Subsidiary Ledgers (Sổ chi tiết) contain complete chronological entries with balanced double-entry accounting.
- **Consistency of Accounting Policies**: Ensure consistency of inventory valuation methods (FIFO, Weighted Average), depreciation methods, and foreign currency accounting throughout the fiscal year; document mandatory disclosures on any policy changes in Form B09-DN.

### 2. Electronic Invoice Compliance & Tax Fraud Screening (Decree 123 / Circular 78)
- **XML Schema & Digital Signature Validation**: Validate all incoming e-invoices against the General Department of Taxation (GDT) Decision 1450/QD-TCT standard XML schema; verify XMLDSig X.509 digital signatures against authorized Certificate Authority (CA) trust chains.
- **Real-Time GDT Portal Verification**: Cross-check invoice issuance status, tax authority verification code (`MCCQT`), invoice symbol (`ký hiệu`), invoice number, and timestamp directly against the national GDT e-invoice portal (`hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn`).
- **Vendor Tax Status & Fraud Screening**: Automatically screen vendor tax identification numbers (MST) against the national taxpayer database (`tracuunnt.gdt.gov.vn`); flag and block invoices from taxpayers with suspended tax status (Trạng thái 03) or taxpayers who have left their registered address (Trạng thái 04) prior to invoice issuance.
- **Form 04/SS-HDDT Discrepancy Tracking**: Monitor vendor-initiated error notices, invoice cancellations, replacements, or adjustments submitted on Form 04/SS-HDDT; freeze payment and disallow input VAT deduction on canceled or replaced invoices until formal replacement is matched.
- **Mandatory Non-Cash Payment Rule**: For invoices with total payment value of 20,000,000 VND or higher (including VAT), verify that payment is executed strictly via bank transfer (Ủy nhiệm chi) from buyer bank account to seller registered bank account; disallow input VAT deduction and CIT expense deductibility on cash payments.

### 3. Deterministic 3-Way Matching & Procurement Controls
- **Deterministic PO-GRN-Invoice Reconciliation**: Match Purchase Order (PO), Goods Receipt Note (GRN / Phiếu nhập kho), and Vendor E-Invoice across SKU numbers, descriptions, quantities, unit prices, payment terms, and applicable VAT rates.
- **Zero Price Variance Tolerance**: Enforce zero unit price variance between PO and Vendor Invoice; block payment processing and require formal PO change order approval for any unapproved price variance.
- **Quantity Variance Tolerance Ceilings**: Enforce strict quantity tolerance limits (zero tolerance for discrete manufactured items; maximum 0.5% tolerance for bulk commodities/liquids); quarantine over-deliveries in unapproved payables.
- **Goods Received Not Invoiced (GRNI) Accruals**: At period cut-off date, identify all warehouse receipts lacking matched vendor invoices; generate provisional GRNI accrual vouchers (Debit TK 152/156, Credit TK 331) at contract price without VAT; automate reversal upon invoice receipt.
- **Goods in Transit (TK 151) Accounting**: Track invoices received and paid/accepted where physical goods have not arrived at warehouse by period cut-off; record to TK 151 (Goods in transit); transfer to warehouse inventory (TK 152/156) upon physical receipt and quality inspection in subsequent period.

### 4. Related-Party Transactions & Net Interest Deduction Cap (Decree 132/2020)
- **Associated Enterprise Identification**: Identify related parties per Decree 132/2020/ND-CP Article 5 criteria (equity participation >= 25%, loans >= 10% equity representing > 50% medium/long-term debt, common executive management, or exclusive distribution control).
- **30% EBITDA Net Interest Limitation**: Calculate Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization (EBITDA) per Decree 132 Article 16; cap deductible net interest expense (Interest Expense minus Interest Income) at strictly 30% of EBITDA for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) computation.
- **Negative or Zero EBITDA Rule**: When EBITDA is zero or negative, net deductible interest expense for the fiscal year is 0 VND; disallow 100% of net interest expense on CIT Return Form 03-1A/TNDN (Box B4).
- **5-Year Carry-Forward Ledger**: Maintain an automated 5-year carry-forward register for disallowed net interest expense; track utilization of carried-forward interest in subsequent years when EBITDA net interest is below 30%.
- **Transfer Pricing Documentation & Safe Harbors**: Verify compliance with Transfer Pricing documentation requirements (Local file Form 02, Master file Form 03, CbCR Form 04) or confirm eligibility for Decree 132 safe harbors (revenue < 50 billion VND and related-party volume < 30 billion VND, or advance pricing agreement).

### 5. Period-End Cut-Off, Adjustments & Account 911 Clearing
- **Subledger Cut-Off & Balance Reconciliations**: Execute period-end cut-offs across AR (TK 131), AP (TK 331), Bank (TK 112), Inventory (TK 15x), Fixed Assets (TK 211/214), Payroll (TK 334), and Statutory Taxes (TK 333); verify that all subledgers reconcile to General Ledger control accounts with zero unexplained variances.
- **Prepaid Expense (TK 242) Allocation**: Automate monthly straight-line amortization of prepaid expenses (tools, office leases, insurance, repair costs) to designated cost centers (TK 642, 641, 154); strictly enforce the statutory 36-month amortization ceiling per Circular 96/2015/TT-BTC.
- **Fixed Asset Depreciation (TK 214)**: Automate monthly depreciation calculations per Circular 45/2013/TT-BTC; verify that useful life parameters align with statutory minimum and maximum brackets in Appendix I of Circular 45; track temporary book-tax depreciation differences.
- **Foreign Currency Year-End Revaluation**: Revalue monetary foreign currency balances (TK 1112, 1122, 131, 331) using commercial bank buying rates for assets and selling rates for liabilities per Circular 200 Article 69; transfer net exchange differences to TK 413 and recognize net gain/loss into TK 515/635; prohibit revaluation of non-monetary items.
- **Revenue Deductions & Net Revenue Closing**: Close trade discounts (TK 5211), sales returns (TK 5212), and sales allowances (TK 5213) into gross sales (TK 511); close net revenue from TK 511 to Account 911; verify zero ending balance in TK 521.
- **Expense Closing to Account 911**: Close cost of goods sold (TK 632), financial expenses (TK 635), selling expenses (TK 641), administrative expenses (TK 642), and other expenses (TK 811) to Account 911; confirm that all temporary expense accounts have zero ending balances.
- **Corporate Income Tax & Profit Transfer**: Calculate accounting profit, apply non-deductible expense adjustments (B1–B14 on Form 03-1A/TNDN), compute current CIT (TK 8211), and transfer net profit or loss from Account 911 to undistributed earnings (TK 4212).
- **Strict Account 911 Zero-Balance Verification**: Enforce a mandatory closing gate: Account 911 must have an ending balance of exactly zero; any non-zero residual balance aborts the closing sequence and triggers audit alerts.

### 6. Statutory Financial Statements Assembly & Balance Equality
- **Statement of Financial Position (B01-DN)**: Assemble Balance Sheet ensuring absolute mathematical equality: Total Assets (Mã số 270) must equal Total Liabilities (Mã số 300) plus Owner's Equity (Mã số 400); verify cash and cash equivalents against bank reconciliations.
- **Statement of Comprehensive Income (B02-DN)**: Validate sequential profit calculations: Net Revenue minus COGS equals Gross Profit; Gross Profit plus Financial Income minus Financial Expense minus Selling Expense minus Admin Expense equals Net Operating Profit; Net Operating Profit plus Other Profit minus CIT equals Profit After Tax.
- **Statement of Cash Flows (B03-DN)**: Verify Cash Flow Statement using Direct or Indirect method; confirm that Net Change in Cash (Mã số 50) equals Ending Cash (Mã số 70) minus Beginning Cash (Mã số 60); reconcile ending cash with Total Cash and Cash Equivalents on Balance Sheet B01-DN.
- **Notes to Financial Statements (B09-DN)**: Prepare comprehensive footnote disclosures covering accounting regime, depreciation schedules, related-party transactions under Decree 132, tax contingencies, and VFRS dual-reporting reconciliation bridges.

### 7. Digital Audit Trail & Segregation of Duties Governance
- **Immutable WORM Audit Trail**: Archive closed period ledgers, trial balances, and financial statements with cryptographic SHA-256 digests; log all accounting events to OCSF 99001 audit event streams with immutable timestamps.
- **Prohibition of Destructive Database Mutations**: Enforce strict write-once-read-many (WORM) principles in accounting databases; prohibit direct SQL UPDATE or DELETE queries on posted journal entries; mandate reverse-and-repost accounting or supplementary adjusting vouchers for all corrections.
- **Segregation of Duties & Non-Discretionary Human Gate**: Enforce strict segregation of duties per Law on Accounting 88/2015/QH13; autonomous AI agents are strictly prohibited from self-closing accounting periods or submitting tax filings; require digital signature tokens and formal sign-off from Chief Accountant and Legal Representative.
- **Statutory Document Retention Governance**: Enforce retention classifications per Law on Accounting (minimum 5 years for management vouchers, minimum 10 years for general ledgers and financial statements, permanent for state enterprise historical records); conduct legal hold verification prior to record disposal.
