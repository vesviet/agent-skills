## Researcher Review Checklist

This reference checklist provides operational verification criteria for deep technical investigation, multi-source triangulation, AI-era source discipline, and factual grounding to meet SOTA 2026–2027 standards. It establishes non-negotiable verification gates across 10-round iterative research depth, anti-circular citation defenses, Citation Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) acyclicity, Chain-of-Verification (CoVe) for YMYL topics, quantitative information density, autonomous Deep Research report auditing, C2PA media provenance verification, and structured contract emission.

### 1. Iterative Research Depth & Execution Discipline (`DEPTH LOCK`)
- **Mandatory 10-Round Exploration in Deep Mode**:
  - investigations marked as `depth_mode: deep` must execute at least ten distinct, documented research rounds exploring varied queries, angles, and primary sources
  - investigations marked as `depth_mode: scoped` mandate at least three rounds and require an explicit `scope_waiver_note` documenting who authorized the reduced scope and why
- **Structured Iteration Logging**:
  - each round logs exact search queries, target URLs visited, and synthesized factual extractions:
    ```json
    {
      "round_index": 4,
      "query_formulation": "vLLM v1 C++ core SPSC ring buffer memory overhead",
      "urls_probed": [
        "https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/tree/main/csrc",
        "https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06180"
      ],
      "discovered_invariants": [
        "POSIX shm SPSC ring buffer bounds scheduling step overhead to <= 0.15ms"
      ]
    }
    ```
  - superficial single-shot research passes lacking multi-angle querying are rejected fail-closed

### 2. AI-Era Source Discipline & Citation DAG Acyclicity (`AI SOURCE LOCK` / `CITATION-DAG-LOCK`)
- **Prohibition of AI Outputs as Primary Sources (`ZERO-AI-PRIMARY-CITATION-LOCK`)**:
  - summaries generated by LLMs, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity answers, or ChatGPT responses classified strictly as Tier 4 query-generation tools; citing AI summaries as primary or secondary factual evidence is strictly prohibited
  - circular reasoning and synthetic echo chambers prevented by tracing all claims to human-authored primary documentation, scientific RFCs, or audited repository commits
- **Citation DAG Mathematical Acyclicity**:
  - all research citations must form a strictly acyclic Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG):
    $$G_{\text{citation}} = (V_{\text{claim}}, E_{\text{cites}}), \quad \text{Cycle}(G_{\text{citation}}) = \emptyset$$
  - circular references ($A \text{ cites } B \text{ cites } A$) or circular cross-citations between AI summaries trigger immediate claim rejection fail-closed:
    ```markdown
    <!-- NEGATIVE ANTI-PATTERN: Circular Citation Echo Chamber (REJECT) -->
    Claim A cites Blog X. Blog X cites Medium Post Y. Medium Post Y cites Claim A.
    
    <!-- POSITIVE: Grounded Directed Citation Chain -->
    Claim A cites Linux Kernel Documentation (kernel.org) -> commit 9a8c...
    ```
- **AI-Citation Mismatch Auditing**:
  - citations provided by AI search tools audited against the actual target URL; citations where the linked page does not substantiate the claim flagged explicitly as `[AI-CITATION MISMATCH]`

### 3. Factual Grounding & Multi-Source Triangulation (`GROUNDING LOCK` / `MULTI-SOURCE-TRIANGULATION-LOCK`)
- **100% Clickable, Live Source URLs**:
  - every material fact, metric, technical constraint, and code standard must cite a clickable, live, and verifiable source URL (asserting HTTP 200 OK)
  - ungrounded inferences labeled explicitly as `[INFERENCE]`; unresolved contradictions labeled `[UNKNOWN]` or `[UNVERIFIED]`
- **Multi-Source Empirical Triangulation**:
  - high-impact factual claims, technical benchmarks, or compliance interpretations must be corroborated across at least **three independent, non-affiliated primary sources**:
    $$\text{Triangulated}(c) \iff |\{s \in \text{PrimarySources}(c) \mid \text{Affiliation}(s) \text{ is pairwise disjoint}\}| \ge 3$$
  - unreconciled contradictions presented with explicit divergent evidence rather than artificially smoothed compromises

### 4. Chain-of-Verification (CoVe) for High-Stakes & YMYL Topics (`COVE-ATOMIC-VERIFICATION-LOCK`)
- **Atomic Sub-Claim Decomposition & Verification**:
  - critical claims affecting security, architecture, legal compliance, or financial accounting decomposed into atomic assertions:
    $$P_{\text{claim}} = \bigwedge_{j=1}^m p_j, \quad \forall j \in \{1,\dots,m\}: \text{Verify}(p_j, \text{PrimarySource}_j) \equiv \text{True}$$
  - verification protocol follows structured Chain-of-Verification (CoVe) execution:
    ```markdown
    | Atomic Proposition ($p_j$) | Verification Question | Primary Source Reference | Verification Status |
    | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | $p_1$: Redis 7.4 supports Lua fencing | Does Redis 7.4 return fencing tokens? | Redis 7.4 Release Notes (redis.io) | CONFIRMED |
    | $p_2$: OceanBase sustains 610k TPS | Is 610k TPS benchmarked on TPC-C? | OceanBase TPC-C Benchmark Report | CONFIRMED |
    | $p_3$: EU AI Act Art 50 live Aug 2026 | Is Article 50 effective Aug 2026? | EUR-Lex Regulation 2024/1689 | CONFIRMED |
    ```

### 5. Information Gain & Quantitative Information Density (`INFORMATION-DENSITY-LOCK`)
- **Information Density Ratio Invariant**:
  - synthesized research briefs must maintain an Information Density metric:
    $$ID = \frac{N_{\text{named entities}} + N_{\text{quantified metrics}}}{N_{\text{total words}}} \ge 0.12$$
  - briefs failing $ID \ge 0.12$ rejected as low-density conversational fluff or AI slop
- **Non-Commodity Value Extraction**:
  - research synthesis must provide actionable Information Gain: identifying unique insights, architectural trade-offs, and empirical findings not present in commodity top 10 search results

### 6. Deep Research Report Auditing & Verification (`DEEP-RESEARCH-VERIFY LOCK`)
- **Independent Source Validation of Autonomous Reports**:
  - autonomous Deep Research outputs (OpenAI Deep Research, Gemini Deep Research, Perplexity Deep Research) treated as draft syntheses rather than authoritative evidence
  - every cited URL and claim inside an automated report must be independently retrieved via `read_url_content` and verified before accepting into the pack knowledge base

### 7. Digital Media & Cryptographic Provenance (`C2PA-CRYPTOGRAPHIC-PROVENANCE-LOCK`)
- **C2PA Content Credentials & Watermark Inspection**:
  - images, diagrams, videos, and charts evaluated for synthetic origin using C2PA Content Credentials or digital watermarking checks:
    ```bash
    c2patool inspect diagram.png --detailed
    ```
  - assets lacking verifiable provenance labeled `[UNVERIFIED — provenance not established]`

### 8. Role Boundary Discipline & Contract Conformance
- **Clean Deliverable Separation**:
  - research outputs emitted strictly as markdown briefs or validating against `contracts/schemas/research-report.json`:
    ```json
    {
      "report_id": "rep-2026-mmo-sota",
      "topic": "MMO 2027 Evasion and Financial Engineering",
      "depth_mode": "deep",
      "rounds_completed": 10,
      "sources_triangulated": 24,
      "cove_verification_passed": true,
      "information_density_score": 0.142
    }
    ```
  - researcher must not author feature tickets (`feature-ticket.json`), architecture ADRs (`adr-spec.json`), or production code

### 9. Operational Failure Modes & Escalation Protocols
- **Information Blackhole Escalation**:
  - topics with zero authoritative primary documentation labeled as `[EVIDENCE-GAP]`; recommend empirical sandbox prototyping to Solution Architect
- **Stale Documentation Traps**:
  - APIs and frameworks deprecated in recent versions flagged immediately; verify git commit logs or official changelogs for release dates
