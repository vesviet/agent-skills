## Researcher Review Checklist

This reference checklist provides operational verification criteria for deep technical investigation, multi-source triangulation, AI-era source discipline, factual grounding, and architectural conformance to meet SOTA 2026–2027 standards. It establishes non-negotiable verification gates across Stanford STORM pre-search persona discovery, 10-round iterative research depth, anti-circular citation defenses, Citation Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) acyclicity, Chain-of-Verification (CoVe) atomic claim decomposition, quantitative information density, autonomous Deep Research report auditing, C2PA media provenance verification, and Draft 2020-12 structured contract emission (`contracts/schemas/research-report.json`).

For full mathematical formulations, algorithmic cycle detection proofs, and architectural details, consult the canonical reference dossier:  
`core/skills/foundation/conduct-research/references/sota-deep-research-architecture.md`.

### 1. Pre-Search Persona Discovery & Stanford STORM Protocol (`STORM-PERSONA-LOCK`)
- **Automated Persona Discovery (4 Mandatory Orthogonal Perspectives)**:
  - prior to formulating or executing search queries, the investigation must instantiate four mandatory expert personas tailored to the domain:
    1. **Skeptical Auditor**: probes unproven assumptions, stress limits, catastrophic edge cases, and circular AI echo chambers.
    2. **Compliance / Regulatory Reviewer**: probes statutory requirements, liabilities, and safety standards (e.g. EU AI Act Regulation 2024/1689, GDPR, TCVN, Decrees, ISO/IEC standards).
    3. **Domain Specialist / Field Practitioner**: probes operational mechanics, empirical p99 benchmarks, runtime bottlenecks, and developer ergonomics.
    4. **Technical Architect / Systems Engineer**: probes interface contracts, data layout, protocol stability, computational complexity ($O(n)$), Citation DAG topology, and backward compatibility.
- **5-Turn Simulated Conversational Interview**:
  - research objectives must undergo a 5-turn simulated dialogue between Perspective-Guided Questioner personas and the Information Provider:
    - *Turn 1*: Perspective Stance Declaration (declaring analytical stance and 3–5 targeted inquiry vectors).
    - *Turn 2*: Multi-Turn Inquiry Execution (in-depth dialogue uncovering failure modes and implementation realities).
    - *Turn 3*: Iterative Query Extraction (extracting empirical, targeted search queries rather than naive keyword phrases).
    - *Turn 4*: Adversarial Challenge & Evidence Filtering (explicitly rejecting marketing summaries or generic AI overviews).
    - *Turn 5*: Outline Construction & Synthesis (synthesizing transcripts into a structured dependency tree).
  - investigations that skip multi-perspective discovery and rely on single-shot or un-decomposed search queries are rejected fail-closed.

### 2. Iterative Research Depth & Execution Discipline (`DEPTH LOCK`)
- **Mandatory 10-Round Exploration in Deep Mode**:
  - investigations marked as `depth_mode: deep` must execute at least ten distinct, documented research rounds exploring varied queries, angles, and primary sources.
  - investigations marked as `depth_mode: scoped` mandate at least three rounds and require an explicit `scope_waiver_note` documenting who authorized the reduced scope and why.
- **Structured Iteration Logging**:
  - each round logs exact search queries, target URLs visited, and synthesized factual extractions:
    ```json
    {
      "round_index": 4,
      "query_formulation": "vLLM v1 C++ core SPSC ring buffer memory overhead",
      "urls_probed": [
        "https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/tree/main/csrc",
        "https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06180"
      ],
      "discovered_invariants": [
        "POSIX shm SPSC ring buffer bounds scheduling step overhead to <= 0.15ms"
      ]
    }
    ```
  - superficial single-shot research passes lacking multi-angle querying are rejected fail-closed.

### 3. AI-Era Source Discipline & Citation DAG Acyclicity (`CITATION-DAG-LOCK` / `ZERO-AI-PRIMARY-CITATION-LOCK`)
- **Prohibition of AI Outputs as Primary Sources (`ZERO-AI-PRIMARY-CITATION-LOCK`)**:
  - summaries generated by LLMs, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity answers, ChatGPT responses, or Claude summaries are classified strictly as Tier 4 query-generation tools; citing AI summaries as primary or secondary factual evidence is strictly prohibited.
  - circular reasoning and synthetic echo chambers are eliminated by tracing all claims to human-authored primary documentation, scientific RFCs, regulatory gazettes, or audited repository commits.
- **Citation DAG Mathematical Acyclicity**:
  - all research citations must form a strictly acyclic Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG):
    $$G = (V, E), \quad V = V_{\text{claim}} \cup V_{\text{source}} \cup V_{\text{evidence}}, \quad \forall v \in V, \text{Path}(v \leadsto v) = \emptyset \iff \text{Cycle}(G) = \emptyset$$
  - circular references ($A \text{ cites } B \text{ cites } A$) or circular cross-citations between AI summaries trigger immediate claim rejection fail-closed:
    ```markdown
    <!-- NEGATIVE ANTI-PATTERN: Circular Citation Echo Chamber (REJECT) -->
    Claim A cites Blog X. Blog X cites Medium Post Y. Medium Post Y cites Claim A.
    
    <!-- POSITIVE: Grounded Directed Citation Chain -->
    Claim A cites Linux Kernel Documentation (kernel.org) -> commit 9a8c...
    ```
- **3-Color DFS Cycle Detection**:
  - graph acyclicity must be programmatically verified using 3-color DFS (White=0, Gray=1, Black=2). Encountering an edge to an active Gray node flags a back-edge cycle and aborts validation.
- **Edge Validation & Self-Loop Rejection**:
  - self-loops ($u \to u$) and edges referencing undefined node IDs are strictly prohibited. Edges must specify typed relationships: `cites`, `corroborates`, `derives_from`, or `contradicts`.
- **AI-Citation Mismatch Auditing**:
  - citations provided by AI search tools audited against the actual target URL; citations where the linked page returns HTTP 404 or does not substantiate the claim are flagged explicitly as `[AI-CITATION MISMATCH]`.

### 4. Factual Grounding & Multi-Source Triangulation (`GROUNDING LOCK` / `MULTI-SOURCE-TRIANGULATION-LOCK`)
- **100% Clickable, Live Source URLs**:
  - every material fact, metric, technical constraint, and code standard must cite a clickable, live, and verifiable source URL (asserting HTTP 200 OK).
  - ungrounded inferences labeled explicitly as `[INFERENCE]`; unresolved contradictions labeled `[UNKNOWN]` or `[UNVERIFIED]`.
- **Multi-Source Empirical Triangulation ($\ge 3$ Independent Primary Sources)**:
  - high-impact factual claims, technical benchmarks, or compliance interpretations must be corroborated across at least **three independent, non-affiliated primary sources**:
    $$\text{Triangulated}(c) \iff |\{s \in \text{PrimarySources}(c) \mid \text{Affiliation}(s) \text{ is pairwise disjoint}\}| \ge 3$$
  - syndication echo chambers (e.g. three news sites republishing the same corporate PR wire) count as exactly one source.
- **Contradiction Preservation Protocol**:
  - conflicting primary sources preserved as divergent edges labeled `relationship: "contradicts"`. Variance explained by environmental variables (kernel, hardware, concurrency) rather than artificially smoothed compromises.

### 5. Chain-of-Verification (CoVe) & Verification Matrix (`COVE-ATOMIC-VERIFICATION-LOCK`)
- **Atomic Sub-Claim Decomposition**:
  - critical claims affecting security, architecture, legal compliance, or financial accounting decomposed into atomic assertions containing single predicates:
    $$P_{\text{claim}} = \bigwedge_{j=1}^m p_j, \quad \forall j \in \{1,\dots,m\}: \text{Verify}(p_j, \text{PrimarySource}_j) \in \{\text{verified}, \text{refuted}, \text{unverifiable}\}$$
- **Context-Isolated Verification Querying**:
  - each atomic proposition $p_j$ queried against primary documentation in isolation, without presenting hypothesis context, eliminating LLM confirmation bias.
- **Structured Verification Matrix Evaluation**:
  - verification protocol follows structured CoVe matrix logging:
    ```markdown
    | Claim ID | Atomic Proposition ($p_j$) | Verification Question | Primary Source URL | Verification Status | Confidence |
    | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | `claim_01` | Redis 7.4 supports Lua fencing | Does Redis 7.4 return fencing tokens? | `https://redis.io/docs/` | `verified` | High |
    | `claim_02` | OceanBase sustains 610k TPS | Is 610k TPS benchmarked on TPC-C? | `https://oceanbase.com/` | `refuted` | High |
    | `claim_03` | EU AI Act Art 50 live Aug 2026 | Is Article 50 effective Aug 2026? | `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/` | `verified` | High |
    ```
  - unverified or refuted claims auto-demoted to `[UNVERIFIED]` or corrected in the synthesis text.

### 6. Quantitative Information Density & Anti-Fluff Governance (`INFORMATION-DENSITY-LOCK`)
- **Information Density Ratio Invariant ($\text{ID} \ge 0.12$)**:
  - substantive research synthesis sections must satisfy the exact quantitative threshold:
    $$\text{ID} = \frac{N_{\text{Named Entities}} + N_{\text{Quantified Metrics}}}{N_{\text{Total Words}}} \ge 0.12$$
  - briefs failing $\text{ID} \ge 0.12$ are rejected fail-closed as low-density conversational fluff.
- **Entity & Metric Taxonomical Rules**:
  - *Named Entities ($N_{\text{Named Entities}}$)*: formal capitalized technical proper nouns, engineering standards (`TCVN 7830:2021`, `RFC 9110`), legal regulations (`EU AI Act 2024/1689`), library identifiers (`Redis 7.4`, `Laravel 9+`).
  - *Quantified Metrics ($N_{\text{Quantified Metrics}}$)*: numerals bound to units, percentages, latencies, throughput rates, timestamps, memory sizes (`0.15ms`, `610k TPS`, `99.99%`, `2026-10-23T02:00:00Z`).
- **The 4 Anti-Fluff Rules**:
  1. Ban generic filler phrases (*"delve into"*, *"pave the way"*, *"testament to"*, *"in today's digital world"*, *"crucial cornerstone"*).
  2. Eliminate weak modals (*"might potentially"*, *"could perhaps"*); use empirical facts or label `[INFERENCE]`.
  3. Enforce fact packing and high noun-phrase density in every paragraph.
  4. Enforce actionable operational specificity (exact file paths, versions, configurations, and flags).

### 7. Deep Research Report Auditing & Verification (`DEEP-RESEARCH-VERIFY LOCK`)
- **Independent Source Validation of Autonomous Reports**:
  - autonomous Deep Research outputs (OpenAI Deep Research, Gemini Deep Research, Perplexity Deep Research) treated strictly as draft syntheses rather than authoritative evidence.
  - every cited URL and claim inside an automated report must be independently retrieved via `read_url_content` and verified before accepting into the repository knowledge base.

### 8. Digital Media & Cryptographic Provenance (`C2PA-CRYPTOGRAPHIC-PROVENANCE-LOCK`)
- **C2PA Content Credentials & Watermark Inspection**:
  - diagrams, charts, screenshots, and PDF assets evaluated for synthetic origin and provenance integrity using C2PA Content Credentials:
    ```bash
    c2patool inspect diagram.png --detailed
    ```
  - status classified into:
    - `signed_valid`: valid X.509 signature rooted in audited CA trust list; SHA-256 hard binding intact.
    - `watermark_detected`: latent model watermark (e.g. SynthID, Digimarc) detected; flagged as AI-generated.
    - `unverified_provenance`: missing manifest or broken signature; labeled `[UNVERIFIED — provenance not established]`.
    - `not_applicable`: textual markdown or raw source code files.

### 9. Role Boundary Discipline & Contract Conformance
- **Clean Deliverable Separation**:
  - research outputs emitted strictly as markdown briefs or validating against `contracts/schemas/research-report.json` (Draft 2020-12).
  - mandatory schema properties: `contract_type: "research-report"`, `objective`, `execution_metrics`, `citation_dag`, `cove_verification_matrix`, `information_density`, `synthesis`, `raw_data_references`, `recommended_next_roles`.
  - researcher must not author feature tickets (`feature-ticket.json`), architecture ADRs (`adr-spec.json`), or production application code.

---

### 10. Operational Failure Modes & Escalation Protocols
- **Information Blackhole Escalation**:
  - topics with zero authoritative primary documentation labeled as `[EVIDENCE-GAP]`; recommend empirical sandbox prototyping to `@technical-architect`.
- **Stale Documentation Traps**:
  - APIs and frameworks deprecated in recent versions flagged immediately; verify git commit logs or official changelogs for release dates.
- **Scope Waiver Evasion**:
  - scoped mode (< 10 rounds) without documented authorization rejected fail-closed.
