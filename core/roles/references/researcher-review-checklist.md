## Researcher Review Checklist

This reference checklist provides operational verification criteria for deep technical investigation, multi-source triangulation, AI-era source discipline, and factual grounding to meet SOTA 2026–2027 standards. It establishes non-negotiable verification gates across 10-round iterative research depth, anti-circular citation defenses, Chain-of-Verification (CoVe) for YMYL topics, information gain assessment, autonomous Deep Research report auditing, C2PA media provenance verification, and structured contract emission.

### 1. Iterative Research Depth & Execution Discipline (`DEPTH LOCK`)
- **Mandatory 10-Round Exploration in Deep Mode**:
  - investigations marked as `depth_mode: deep` must execute at least ten distinct, documented research rounds exploring varied queries, angles, and sources
  - investigations marked as `depth_mode: scoped` mandate at least three rounds and require an explicit `scope_waiver_note` documenting who narrowed the scope and why
- **Iterative Question Decomposition**:
  - initial research objectives decomposed into atomic sub-questions executed in structured logical sequence rather than superficial single-shot queries

### 2. AI-Era Source Discipline & Anti-Circular Citation Defense (`AI SOURCE LOCK` / `HALLUCINATION FLAG`)
- **Prohibition of AI Outputs as Primary/Secondary Sources**:
  - summaries generated by LLMs, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity answers, or ChatGPT responses classified strictly as Tier 4 query-generation tools; citing AI summaries as factual primary or secondary evidence is strictly prohibited
  - circular reasoning and synthetic echo chambers prevented by tracing all claims to human-authored primary documentation, scientific papers, or authoritative records
- **AI-Citation Mismatch Auditing**:
  - citations provided by AI search tools audited against the actual target URL; citations where the linked page does not substantiate the claim flagged explicitly as `[AI-CITATION MISMATCH]`

### 3. Factual Grounding & Primary Source Verification (`GROUNDING LOCK`)
- **100% Clickable, Verifiable Source URLs**:
  - every material fact, metric, technical constraint, and code standard must cite a clickable, live, and verifiable source URL
  - ungrounded inferences labeled explicitly as `[INFERENCE]`; unresolved contradictions labeled `[UNKNOWN]` or `[UNVERIFIED]`
- **Credibility Hierarchy Adherence**:
  - evidence prioritized according to the strict source hierarchy:
    1. Primary (official documentation, source code repositories, standards RFCs, audited reports)
    2. Secondary (peer-reviewed articles, verified technical case studies, industry benchmarks)
    3. Tertiary (general tech blogs, aggregation articles)

### 4. Chain-of-Verification (CoVe) for High-Stakes & YMYL Topics (`CoVe LOCK`)
- **Atomic Sub-Claim Decomposition**:
  - critical claims affecting security, architecture, legal compliance, or financial accounting decomposed into atomic assertions
- **Independent Verification Step**:
  - each atomic sub-claim independently verified against original raw source text; verified vs unverified claims segregated in the final synthesis

### 5. Information Gain & Differentiating Value Extraction (`INFORMATION GAIN GATE`)
- **Non-Commodity Value Extraction**:
  - research synthesis must provide actionable Information Gain: identifying unique insights, architectural trade-offs, and empirical findings not present in commodity top-5 search results
  - document `AI_coverage_gap`: explicitly highlighting where current AI models and public summaries provide incorrect, outdated, or superficial guidance

### 6. Deep Research Report Auditing & Verification (`DEEP-RESEARCH-VERIFY LOCK`)
- **Independent Source Validation of Autonomous Reports**:
  - autonomous Deep Research outputs (e.g. OpenAI Deep Research, Gemini Deep Research, Perplexity Deep Research) treated as draft syntheses rather than authoritative evidence
  - every cited URL and claim inside an automated report must be independently retrieved and verified before accepting into the pack knowledge base

### 7. Digital Media & Asset Provenance Verification (`PROVENANCE LOCK`)
- **C2PA Content Credentials & Watermark Inspection**:
  - images, diagrams, videos, and audio assets evaluated for synthetic origin using C2PA Content Credentials or digital watermarking checks
  - assets lacking verifiable provenance labeled `[UNVERIFIED — provenance not established]`

### 8. Role Boundary Discipline & Contract Conformance
- **Clean Deliverable Separation**:
  - research outputs emitted strictly as markdown briefs or validating against `contracts/schemas/research-report.json`
  - researcher must not author feature tickets (`feature-ticket.json`), architecture ADRs (`adr-spec.json`), or production code

### 9. Triangulation & Contradiction Resolution Standards (`TRIANGULATION LOCK`)
- **Multi-Source Empirical Corroboration**:
  - contentious assertions verified across $\ge 3$ independent primary sources representing differing industry implementations
  - unreconciled contradictions presented with explicit divergent evidence rather than artificially smoothed compromises

### 10. Operational Failure Modes & Escalation Protocols
- **Information Blackhole Escalation**:
  - topics with zero authoritative primary documentation labeled as `[EVIDENCE-GAP]`; recommend empirical sandbox prototyping to Solution Architect
- **Stale Documentation Traps**:
  - APIs and frameworks deprecated in recent versions flagged immediately; verify git commit logs or official changelogs for release dates
