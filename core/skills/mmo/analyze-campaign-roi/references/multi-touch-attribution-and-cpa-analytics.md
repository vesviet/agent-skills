# Multi-Touch Attribution & True ROI Analytics (2026)

## 1. True ROI Financial Modeling

In high-velocity performance marketing, raw advertising ROAS ($\text{Revenue} / \text{AdSpend}$) distorts financial health by ignoring operational friction. True ROI aggregates all cost vectors:

$$\text{True Cost} = \text{AdSpend} + \text{ProxyBandwidth} + \text{AI\_API\_Tokens} + (\text{AccountDieRate} \times \text{ReplaceCost}) + \text{ClawbackEscrow}$$

$$\text{True Net Profit} = \text{GrossRevenue} - \text{True Cost}$$

$$\text{True ROI} = \frac{\text{True Net Profit}}{\text{True Cost}}$$

## 2. LTV Cohort Retention Curves (Day 30/60/90)

For recurring subscription and trial offers, evaluating single-day CPA causes premature abandonment of profitable funnels:
- $\text{LTV}_{30} = \text{CPA}_0 + (r_1 \cdot V_{\text{rebill}})$
- $\text{LTV}_{60} = \text{LTV}_{30} + (r_2 \cdot V_{\text{rebill}})$
- $\text{LTV}_{90} = \text{LTV}_{60} + (r_3 \cdot V_{\text{rebill}})$

Where $r_i$ represents the retention probability of the cohort at month $i$, and $V_{\text{rebill}}$ is the subscription net payout.

## 3. Blended ROAS & Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER)

- **Blended ROAS**: $\frac{\text{Total Net Revenue}}{\text{Total Ad Spend Across All Channels}}$
- **Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER)**: $\frac{\text{Total Net Revenue}}{\text{Total Marketing \& Operational Expenses}}$
- Reconciles dark social conversions, iOS 18+ modeled attributions (AdAttributionKit), and delayed cross-device paths.

## 4. Multi-Tier Affiliate Economics & Clawback Reserves

- **Tier 1**: Direct payout per acquisition ($CPA$).
- **Tier 2**: Master affiliate override percentage ($3\% \text{--} 10\%$).
- **Volume Milestones**: Tiered bonuses paid when monthly conversion volume crosses contractual thresholds.
- **Clawback Escrow**: Maintain $3\% \text{--} 5\%$ reserve deduction against chargebacks and affiliate network refund audits.
