# Design Learning Plan

Use this skill to structure a personalized or generalized study plan, ensuring strict alignment with the Vietnam Ministry of Education and Training (MOET) standards for middle school (THCS), with special attention to students transitioning from Grade 6 to Grade 7.

## Core Rules

- Always map the plan to the 35-week school year format (Học kỳ I: 18 tuần, Học kỳ II: 17 tuần).
- Organize study blocks based on standard 45-minute periods (Tiết học).
- For the Grade 6 to 7 transition, explicitly include time for consolidating foundational learning habits (self-study, note-taking) as the curriculum becomes more abstract and demanding (e.g., Natural Sciences, History & Geography).
- Do not overwhelm the learner; respect cognitive load limits and avoid university-level theory.

## Suggested Process

1. **Analyze Requirements**: Identify the subject, target grade (focusing on Lớp 6, Lớp 7), time constraints, and specific goals (e.g., Ôn tập hè, Kiểm tra giữa kì).
2. **Determine the Scope**: Select the chapters and concepts based on the official textbook (Chương trình GDPT 2018).
3. **Sequence the Learning**: Order topics logically. For 6-to-7 transition, start by reviewing Grade 6 core concepts before introducing Grade 7 new theory.
4. **Allocate Time**: Assign realistic 45-minute blocks. Balance between theory (lý thuyết), practice (bài tập), and review (ôn tập).
5. **Set Milestones**: Define checkpoints (Kiểm tra 15 phút, Kiểm tra 1 tiết/Giữa kì, Cuối kì).

## Checklist

- [ ] Target audience is explicitly defined (e.g., Học sinh chuẩn bị lên lớp 7).
- [ ] Timeline matches the MOET school year structure and 45-minute periods.
- [ ] Plan includes specific review checkpoints (Giữa kì, Cuối kì).
- [ ] Curriculum aligns with the 2018 GDPT standards.
- [ ] Built-in time for consolidating self-study skills.
