# Design Learning Plan

Use this skill to structure a personalized or generalized study plan, ensuring strict alignment with the Vietnam Ministry of Education and Training (MOET) standards for middle school (THCS), covering all four grades (Lớp 6, 7, 8, 9).

## Core Rules

- Always map the plan to the 35-week school year format (Học kỳ I: 18 tuần, Học kỳ II: 17 tuần).
- Organize study blocks based on standard 45-minute periods (Tiết học).
- For grade transition plans (6→7, 7→8, 8→9), explicitly include time for consolidating foundational habits from the previous grade before introducing new abstract or advanced content.
- Do not overwhelm the learner; respect cognitive load limits appropriate to the student’s grade level.

## Suggested Process

1. **Analyze Requirements**: Identify the subject, target grade (Lớp 6, 7, 8, or 9), time constraints, and specific goals (e.g., Ôn tập hè, Kiểm tra giữa kì, chuẩn bị thi lớp 10).
2. **Determine the Scope**: Select the chapters and concepts based on the official textbook (Chương trình GDPT 2018) for the target grade.
3. **Sequence the Learning**: Order topics logically. For grade transition plans, start by reviewing the prior grade's key concepts before introducing new theory.
4. **Allocate Time**: Assign realistic 45-minute blocks. Balance between theory (lý thuyết), practice (bài tập), and review (ôn tập).
5. **Set Milestones**: Define checkpoints (Kiểm tra 15 phút, Kiểm tra 1 tiết/Giữa kì, Cuối kì).

## Checklist

- [ ] Target grade (Lớp 6, 7, 8, or 9) and specific goal (semester plan, exam prep, grade transition) are explicitly defined.
- [ ] Timeline matches the MOET school year structure and 45-minute periods.
- [ ] Plan includes grade-appropriate review checkpoints (Giữa kì, Cuối kì).
- [ ] Curriculum aligns with the 2018 GDPT standards for the target grade.
- [ ] Built-in time for consolidating learning habits or bridging previous grade gaps.
