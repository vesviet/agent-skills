# Grade And Review

Use this skill to assess a student's completed exercise, assign scores based on the Vietnamese 10-point scale, and provide constructive, psychologically supportive feedback tailored for middle schoolers (especially 6th/7th graders).

## Core Rules

- Always score out of 10. Round scores to the nearest 0.25 (e.g., 7.25, 8.5) as per MOET regulations.
- For students transitioning from Grade 6 to 7, prioritize encouraging feedback over harsh criticism, as they are adjusting to a heavier academic load.
- Categorize performance internally if needed (Tốt/Giỏi: 8-10, Khá: 6.5-7.9, Đạt/Trung bình: 5-6.4, Chưa đạt/Yếu: < 5).
- Never just say "Sai" (Wrong) without explaining the exact step where the logic failed.

## Suggested Process

1. **Review the Submission**: Compare the student's work step-by-step against the "Đáp án & Thang điểm" (Rubric).
2. **Score the Work**: Award partial points (0.25 or 0.5) for correct intermediate steps, even if the final answer is wrong. Calculate the final score / 10.
3. **Draft Student Feedback**: Address the student directly. Highlight what they did well (Điểm tốt) before pointing out mistakes (Lỗi sai).
4. **Draft Parent Feedback (Optional)**: Summarize the student's current proficiency and suggest home support if requested.
5. **Provide a Correction Guide (Hướng dẫn sửa lỗi)**: Offer detailed, step-by-step hints so the student can arrive at the correct answer themselves.

## Checklist

- [ ] Final score uses the 10-point scale (rounded to 0.25).
- [ ] Partial credit is awarded for constructed-response (Tự luận) questions.
- [ ] Feedback tone is highly encouraging and patient (suitable for 11-12 year olds).
- [ ] Specific corrections are provided for every mistake.
- [ ] Terminology strictly aligns with Vietnamese educational standards.
