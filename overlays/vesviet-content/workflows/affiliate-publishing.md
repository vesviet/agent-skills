# Affiliate Publishing Workflow (`learn`)

This workflow governs the creation and publication of affiliate and hybrid SEO content for the `learn` repository. It enforces the 2026 E-E-A-T standards against AI spam and parasitic SEO.

## Content Categorization
Before drafting, categorize the target page:
1. **Money Pages**: High-intent conversion pages (Best X for Y, X vs Y, Alternative to X, Deep Reviews).
2. **Supporting Articles**: Educational content (How-to, Common mistakes, Setup guide).
3. **Trust Pages**: Foundational compliance pages (About, Contact, Editorial policy, Affiliate disclosure, How we test).

## 4-Step Production Workflow

### Step 1: Niche & Blueprint Strategy (`seo-analyst`)
- Define the cluster. Ensure there are enough Supporting articles to build authority for the Money pages.
- Verify that the niche has a clear commercial intent with at least 2 viable affiliate networks/merchants.

### Step 2: Experience-Driven Drafting (`content-writer`)
- Do NOT use AI to mass-generate content without human experience.
- Include explicit Evaluation Criteria, Pros/Cons, "Best For" and "Not For" sections in reviews.
- Synthesize real data, testing results, or rigorous comparative research.

### Step 3: Affiliate Compliance & On-Page SEO (`seo-analyst`)
- **Disclosure**: Ensure the affiliate disclosure is prominently placed near recommendations.
- **Link Tagging**: Apply `rel="sponsored"` to all outbound affiliate links.
- **Structured Data**: Add `Product` or `Review` schema where applicable.

### Step 4: Hybrid Distribution (`content-manager`)
- Publish the SEO-optimized article as the anchor.
- Repurpose key insights into short-form formats (TikTok, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts) to drive social commerce traffic back to the anchor article.
