# Publishing Cadence & Operational Runbook

Rules for the daily operation of the SEO Publishing overlay, focusing on cadence limits, scheduling, and standard per-post baselines.

## 1. Dual-Site Sprint Cadence

When the sprint is active across both the `Lease in Vietnam` and `Máy Lạnh Treo Tường` portfolios, the following volume limits apply:
- **Maximum Output:** 2 posts per day total.
- **Distribution:** Exactly 1 post for Site A, and 1 post for Site B.
- **Cadence Window:** Operate on a rolling **7-day Topic Board** cycle.

## 2. Per-Post Baseline Requirements

Every post passing through this publishing overlay must satisfy three core optimization layers before going live:

### A. GEO / AEO Layer (Generative & Answer Engine Optimization)
- **Answer-First Structure:** The introduction MUST immediately answer the core user intent within the first 60 words, explicitly formatted (e.g., `**Answer-first:**`).
- **Fact Density:** Replace generic filler ("it is very fast") with concrete specs and data ("it processes at 120Hz").
- **Query Fan-Out:** Structure headings to anticipate and answer logical follow-up questions (People Also Ask / LLM follow-ups).

### B. E-E-A-T & Trust Layer
- **Experience Proof:** The draft MUST contain explicit evidence of first-hand experience (e.g., "In our testing of the 2026 model...", "When we visited the property...").
- **Author Entity:** The author must be clearly declared and linked to an established entity page.

### C. Traditional SEO Layer
- Primary keyword in title, URL slug, and meta description.
- Minimum of 3 internal links.
- Required Schema/JSON-LD specifications must be documented for the Frontend Developer.
