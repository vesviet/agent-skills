# Affiliate Publishing Workflow (`learn`)

This workflow governs the creation and publication of affiliate and hybrid SEO content for the `learn` repository. It enforces the 2026 E-E-A-T standards against AI spam and parasitic SEO.

## Content Categorization
Before drafting, categorize the target page:
1. **Money Pages**: High-intent conversion pages (Best X for Y, X vs Y, Alternative to X, Deep Reviews).
2. **Supporting Articles**: Educational content (How-to, Common mistakes, Setup guide).
3. **Trust Pages**: Foundational compliance pages (About, Contact, Editorial policy, Affiliate disclosure, How we test).

## 4-Step Production Workflow

### Step 1: Niche & Blueprint Strategy (`seo-analyst`)
- Define the cluster. Ensure there are enough Supporting articles to build authority for the Money pages.
- Verify that the niche has a clear commercial intent with at least 2 viable affiliate networks/merchants.

### Step 2: Experience-Driven Drafting (`content-writer`)
- Do NOT use AI to mass-generate content without human experience.
- Include explicit Evaluation Criteria, Pros/Cons, "Best For" and "Not For" sections in reviews.
- Synthesize real data, testing results, or rigorous comparative research.

### Step 3: Affiliate Compliance & On-Page SEO (`seo-analyst`)
- **Disclosure**: Ensure the affiliate disclosure is prominently placed near recommendations.
- **Link Tagging**: Apply `rel="sponsored"` to all outbound affiliate links.
- **Structured Data**: Add `Product` or `Review` schema where applicable.

### Step 4: Hybrid Distribution (`content-manager`)
- Publish the SEO-optimized article as the anchor.
- Repurpose key insights into short-form formats (TikTok, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts) to drive social commerce traffic back to the anchor article.

### Failure Modes

- **Money page without supporting cluster**: a high-intent Money page is published without the required Supporting articles to build authority. **Mitigation:** Step 1 requires a cluster blueprint with at least 2 Supporting articles; reject the Money page when the cluster is not built.
- **AI mass-generated content without human experience**: a draft is generated by AI without the required firsthand experience, evaluation criteria, or pros/cons sections. **Mitigation:** Step 2 forbids mass generation; reject the draft when the E-E-A-T elements are missing.
- **Affiliate disclosure missing or hidden**: an outbound affiliate link ships without the required disclosure. **Mitigation:** Step 3 requires the disclosure near recommendations; reject the publish when the disclosure is hidden.
- **Missing `rel="sponsored"` on affiliate link**: an outbound affiliate link ships with the wrong attribute. **Mitigation:** Step 3 enforces `rel="sponsored"`; reject the publish when the attribute is missing.
- **Anchor article absent in distribution**: the SEO article is published but the short-form distribution is not run. **Mitigation:** Step 4 requires the hybrid distribution to be planned and executed; reject the workflow when the distribution is missing.

### Output Contracts

When this workflow produces a structured handoff, emit:

- **`contracts/schemas/seo-content-brief.json`** from Step 1, capturing the niche, the cluster, the supporting articles, and the commercial intent.
- **`contracts/schemas/content-handoff.json`** (or markdown frontmatter block) from Step 3, capturing the publish event, the affiliate disclosure placement, and the structured data decision.
- **`contracts/schemas/release-notes.json`** (or frontmatter block) from Step 4, capturing the distribution channels and the repurposed short-form content.
